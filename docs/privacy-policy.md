# Privacy Policy for Time Tracker

**Last updated:** 30 July 2026

Time Tracker ("the app") is a personal timesheet app that lets you record your
own working hours. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what
control you have over it.

If you have questions, contact **kunalgupta9806@gmail.com**.

---

## What we collect

**Account information — from Google Sign-In**

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

- Your email address
- Your name
- Your Google profile picture URL
- A unique account identifier

We do not receive your Google password, and we cannot access your Gmail,
contacts, files, or any other Google service.

**Timesheet data — created by you**

- Dates you worked
- Clock-in and clock-out times, and calculated hours
- Day markers you choose (Work from Home, On Leave)
- Any notes you type against a day

**What we do not collect**

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party
SDKs of that kind. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera,
microphone, device identifiers for advertising, or your browsing activity.

---

## Why we collect it

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Email, name, account identifier | To create your account, sign you in, and keep your timesheet separate from everyone else's |
| Profile picture URL | To display your name and picture in the app |
| Timesheet entries and notes | To provide the app's core function — recording and reporting your hours |

We do **not** use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

---

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using [Supabase](https://supabase.com), a hosted database
provider, and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Each account's records are isolated at the database level using row-level
security. Signed-in users can only read and write their own entries.

**Local storage on your device.** So the app works without a connection, a copy
of your recent entries and any unsent changes is kept on your device. This
local copy is removed when you sign out (once pending changes have uploaded) or
when you delete your account.

---

## Who your data is shared with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Data is shared only with:

- **Supabase** — our database and authentication provider, which stores the data on our behalf
- **Google** — solely to authenticate you when you choose "Sign in with Google"

We may disclose information if legally required to do so.

**Note on exports.** The app lets you export your own timesheet as a PDF or
JSON file. Once you share an exported file, that copy is outside the app's
control and this policy no longer governs it.

---

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your
account, your profile and all timesheet entries are deleted immediately and
permanently. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

---

## Your rights and choices

**Delete your account.** In the app, go to **Dashboard → Delete My Account**.
This permanently erases your account, your profile, and every timesheet entry.

**Delete your entries but keep your account.** Use **Dashboard → Erase All Data**.

**Export your data.** Use **Dashboard → Export PDF** or **Export JSON** at any
time to obtain a copy of your records.

**Access or correction.** You can view and edit all of your data directly in the
app. For any other request, email **kunalgupta9806@gmail.com**.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as
the GDPR or CCPA — including access, correction, deletion, and portability. The
in-app controls above cover these, and you can email us for anything else.

---

## Children

Time Tracker is intended for use by working adults and is not directed at
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you
believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we
will delete it.

---

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Access is protected by Google
authentication, and database row-level security ensures one account cannot read
another's records.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a
breach affects your data, we will notify affected users.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above
and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
