Time Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 July 2026

Time Tracker is a timesheet app that lets you record your own working hours. This policy explains what the app collects, why it collects it, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

Account information, from Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your email address
  • Your name
  • Your Google profile picture URL
  • A unique account identifier

We never receive your Google password, and we cannot access your Gmail, contacts, files, or any other Google service.

Timesheet data, created by you

  • Dates you worked
  • Clock-in and clock-out times, and the calculated hours
  • Day markers you choose — Work from Home, On Leave
  • Any notes you type against a day

What we do not collect

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or your browsing activity.

Why we collect it

DataPurpose
Email, name, account identifier To create your account, sign you in, and keep your timesheet separate from everyone else's
Profile picture URL To display your name and picture in the app
Timesheet entries and notes To provide the app's core function — recording and reporting your hours

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a hosted database provider, and is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Each account's records are isolated at the database level using row-level security. A signed-in user can only read and write their own entries.

A copy stays on your device So the app works without a connection, your recent entries and any unsent changes are kept on your device. That local copy is removed when you sign out, once pending changes have uploaded, or when you delete your account.

Who your data is shared with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores the data on our behalf
  • Google — solely to authenticate you when you choose "Sign in with Google"

We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so.

Exported files leave the app You can export your timesheet as a PDF or JSON file. Once you share an exported file, that copy is outside the app's control and this policy no longer governs it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and all timesheet entries are deleted immediately and permanently. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Your rights and choices

Everything below is available inside the app, with no need to contact us:

Dashboard → Delete My Account Permanently erases your account, your profile, and every timesheet entry.
Dashboard → Erase All Data Deletes all your entries but keeps your account active.
Dashboard → Export PDF or Export JSON Obtain a copy of your records at any time.

You can view and edit all of your data directly in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including access, correction, deletion, and portability. The in-app controls above cover these — email us for anything else.

Children

Time Tracker is intended for working adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Access is protected by Google authentication, and database row-level security ensures one account cannot read another's records.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will notify affected users.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
